Questions, answered

The things people ask first.

Is it really free to start?

Yes. The free tier gives you a daily allowance to work with the whole team, CV help, job-advert decoding, interview prep and Juno's support, and we never ask for card details to start.

Do I have to take out a subscription?

No, there are no subscriptions at all. The only paid option is a one-off Week Pass at £9.99: everything unlocked for seven days, then it simply ends. No monthly or annual plans, no auto-renew, no card kept on file, nothing to cancel.

Will my CV sound like it was written by AI?

No, and this is a design principle, not a promise. Everything is built from your real experience, in your own words and rhythm. The system asks questions until it has the truth, then writes the way you would on your best day. Nothing is invented, and corporate ghost-speak is banned by rule.

How is this different from CV builders and career toolkits?

Toolkits organise your tasks; this is a team that knows your story. Your five specialists remember everything between conversations, tell you the truth about your chances before you apply, coach you like people rather than forms, and, unlike every toolkit, stay with you after you're hired.

Is my data safe?

Your career history belongs to you. You can export or delete everything at any time, we never sell your information, and the app is built to UK GDPR standards from day one.

I'm a careers adviser, can I use this with clients?

Yes, Adviser Mode is built for exactly that: one private file per client, plain-language printable outputs, and explanations you can pass on so your client learns. Email admin@cultureclarity.uk and mention your service; we're planning pilots with job centres and careers services.
