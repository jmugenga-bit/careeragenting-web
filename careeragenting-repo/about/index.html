About us

About Career Agenting System

Career Agenting System is a free career team for the UK — five specialists that help you build a CV, apply for jobs, prepare for interviews, and stay supported through the ups and downs. Here's who's behind it.

What we do

Career Agenting System brings together five specialists — Wren for your CV, Scout for job applications, Ace for interviews, Juno for the emotional side, and Cedar for growing once you're in a role. It's free to start, with no sign-up, and it's built specifically for the UK job market.

Why we exist

Good career help shouldn't depend on what you can afford. A private career coach can cost a great deal, and free public services — valuable as they are — are stretched. We built Career Agenting System so that anyone in the UK, whatever their circumstances, can get genuinely useful help with their CV, applications and interviews, and a steady presence through a hard process. You can read more about that on our free career help page.

How we keep our advice honest

We take accuracy seriously. Our guidance is UK-specific, kept factual and dated, and points to trusted public sources like the National Careers Service and GOV.UK. It's general career guidance — not legal, financial or benefits advice — and where a topic touches wellbeing, we signpost real support. You can read our full editorial standards, and see plainly how the service is free and how we make money.
