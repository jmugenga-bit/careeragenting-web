United States · Arizona

Just laid off in Arizona? Here's your playbook.

Phoenix is booming — TSMC and Intel chip fabs, fintech, aerospace and a rush of new residents. But Arizona keeps its worker protections light. So this guide is honest and practical: file fast, know your rights, and get your résumé working for a market that's hiring hard in the right sectors. Free to use.

The first 72 hours after a layoff

  • Your final paycheck. In Arizona, if you're laid off or fired your final wages are due within seven working days or the end of the next pay period, whichever is sooner. (If you quit, they're due by the next regular payday.)
  • File for unemployment right away. Arizona pays up to $320 a week for up to 24 weeks (26 if the state's jobless rate is high enough). File at des.az.gov.
  • Sort out health cover through HealthCare.gov (a job loss lets you enroll outside open enrollment) or continue your plan via COBRA.
  • Don't rush to sign severance — read it, and have it reviewed, first.

Your rights when you're let go in Arizona

  • At-will, with limits. Arizona is at-will, but you can't be fired for an unlawful reason like discrimination or retaliation, handled by the EEOC and the Arizona Civil Rights Division.
  • Layoff notice. Arizona has no state WARN law, but the federal WARN Act can require larger employers (100+ staff) to give 60 days' notice of a mass layoff or plant closing.
  • No state pay-transparency law and no required severance.

General information, not legal advice. For your situation, use the official Arizona resources below or an employment attorney.

Money and leverage while you search

  • Claim unemployment the day you're let go (above); report any part-time or gig income and you may still get a reduced benefit.
  • Arizona doesn't require employers to post pay. Research the market rate yourself — comparable postings, Glassdoor, Levels.fyi — and ask for the range early, especially in the fast-moving chip and fintech sectors where demand is high.

Your résumé for the Arizona market

Arizona hiring — semiconductors, fintech, aerospace — is ATS-first. Mirror the posting's exact keywords, keep the formatting clean, lead with achievements and metrics, and skip the photo, birth date and age.

Frequently asked questions

How much is unemployment in Arizona, and for how long?

Arizona pays up to $320 a week for up to 24 weeks (26 if the state's jobless rate is high enough). File at des.az.gov.

When do I get my final paycheck in Arizona?

If you're laid off or fired, within seven working days or the end of the next pay period, whichever is sooner. If you quit, by the next regular payday.

Do Arizona employers have to post the salary?

No. Arizona has no state pay-transparency law, so research the market rate yourself and ask for the range early.

Does Arizona have a state WARN law for layoffs?

No &mdash; but the federal WARN Act can require larger employers (100+ staff) to give 60 days' advance notice of a mass layoff or plant closing.

Is this legal or financial advice?

No. It's general information, current as of August 2026 and based on the official Arizona sources on this page. For your own situation, check those sources or a qualified attorney or advisor.
