Indeed and Career Agenting System — find the job, then win it

Indeed is one of the best places to find and apply for jobs in the US — that's not in doubt. What it doesn't do is write a standout resume, tailor each application, or practice you for the interview. That's where a free career team comes in.

Indeed is the largest job board in the US, with a huge number of live openings, one-click apply, company reviews and salary data. For finding and applying to roles, it's excellent — and free.

What Indeed does well

  • A huge volume of live US openings
  • Fast, easy applications and job alerts
  • Company reviews and salary information

What a job board doesn't do

  • It lists jobs, but it doesn't write or tailor your resume to a specific posting.
  • It doesn't run mock interviews or predict the questions you'll be asked.
  • It can't help with the human side — the rejections and the waiting.

Frequently asked questions

Is Career Agenting System an alternative to Indeed?

Not exactly — they do different jobs. Indeed is where you find and apply to openings; Career Agenting System helps you win them with a tailored resume, application help and interview practice. Use both.

How do I stand out when applying on Indeed?

Tailor your resume to each posting rather than sending the same one everywhere. Scout decodes the posting so you can mirror what the employer is asking for, and Wren rewrites your resume around it — free.

Can it help me prep for an interview I got through Indeed?

Yes — Ace predicts likely questions for the role and runs full mock interviews so you can practice out loud. Free, no sign-up.
