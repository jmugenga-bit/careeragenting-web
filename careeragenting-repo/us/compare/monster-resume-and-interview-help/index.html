Monster and Career Agenting System — apply, then actually land it

Monster has been matching Americans to jobs for decades — search, alerts, and resume posting. What it doesn't do is tailor your resume to each role or rehearse you for the interview. That's where a free career team helps.

Monster is one of the oldest US job boards, with job search, email alerts, resume posting and some career advice. For a steady stream of openings across industries, it does the job.

What Monster does well

  • A long-standing board across many industries
  • Resume posting so employers can find you
  • Job alerts and search filters

What a board leaves to you

  • Posting a generic resume isn't the same as tailoring it to a role.
  • It doesn't predict your interview questions or run a mock.
  • It can't help with the confidence and the waiting.
