How to use it

  1. Type one task or responsibility in your own words.
  2. Add a number or result if you have one, even a rough figure.
  3. Press the button for achievement-style rewrites, strong verbs to try, and a prompt for the metric that makes it land.

Why achievements beat duties

A job description lists duties; a great CV shows achievements, what changed because you were there. The formula is simple: a strong action verb, what you did, and the result (ideally a number). “Responsible for social media” becomes “Grew the team’s following by 40% in six months.” Recruiters and screening software both respond to that shift.
