Frequently asked questions

Is this CV keyword checker free?

Yes, completely free, with no sign-up. It runs entirely in your browser, so nothing you paste is uploaded, stored or shared.

Is this an ATS checker?

It checks how well your CV matches the key terms in a specific job advert, which is the core of what applicant-tracking systems and recruiters look for. For a full rewrite tailored to the advert, our free app does that for you.

How do I improve my keyword match?

Add the missing terms that genuinely apply to you, use the advert's exact wording, and put the most important ones near the top of your CV. Don't add anything that isn't true.

Does it store my CV or the job advert?

No. Everything runs locally in your browser and nothing is sent anywhere.
