    Frequently asked questions

    Is this interview question generator free?

    Yes — free with no sign-up. Pick a category to see common UK questions for that type of interview.

    What are the most common interview questions?

    'Tell me about yourself', 'Why do you want this job?', and competency questions like 'Tell me about a time you worked in a team' are among the most common in the UK. Choose a category above for more.

    How do I answer competency questions?

    Use a situation → action → result structure: set the scene briefly, focus on what you did, and finish with the outcome. Prepare six to eight specific examples in advance.

    Can I get questions for a specific job?

    Yes — the free app predicts likely questions for your exact role and employer and runs mock interviews, which this simple generator doesn't do.
