For work coaches & careers advisers

If you help people find work — as a Jobcentre Plus work coach, a careers adviser, or a volunteer at a job club or charity — here's a free set of AI-powered tools you're welcome to share. They're built to extend the help you already give, not replace it.

Good career help, without the waiting list

One-to-one careers support is one of the most powerful things you offer — and one of the most stretched. There's never enough time to sit with everyone, rebuild every CV, and run mock interviews as often as people need. That's exactly the gap Career Agenting System is built to fill: a free, AI-powered career team your clients can use any time, on a phone, with no sign-up — so they arrive at your appointments further along, and keep making progress between them.

It doesn't replace you. A person still needs someone in their corner who understands their situation. What the AI does is the patient, repeatable work — tailoring a CV to an advert, practising interview answers, explaining a gap with confidence — as often as they like, for free.

Frequently asked questions

Is this an official Jobcentre Plus or government tool?

No. Career Agenting System is an independent service from Culture & Clarity Limited. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by DWP, Jobcentre Plus, the NHS or any government body, and it signposts official services like the National Careers Service.

Is it really free for the people I support?

Yes — the core help is free to start with no sign-up. There's an optional £9.99 Week Pass for heavier use, but the essentials stay free, which matters for the people who most need support.

Will it replace the work coaches and advisers?

No — it's designed to extend your help, not replace it. It handles the patient, repeatable work (CV tailoring, interview practice) so people make progress between appointments, while you provide the human judgement and support only a person can.

Can my team or organisation partner with you?

Yes — if you run a careers service, job club, college or charity, please get in touch via our contact page. We're glad to talk.
