Free, honest career help — for everyone

Not everyone can afford a career coach, and stretched public services can't always give you the time you need. This is free help, available any time — for students, job seekers, and anyone getting back to work.

WWren · CV SScout · applications AAce · interviews JJuno · support CCedar · growing in the job

Good career help shouldn't depend on what you can afford. A private career coach can cost a great deal — often out of reach exactly when money is tightest and help matters most. Free public services like the National Careers Service and Jobcentre Plus do valuable work, but they are stretched, and it isn't always easy to get the time and one-to-one attention you need, when you need it.

Career Agenting System exists to help close that gap. It's free to start, with no sign-up, and it's there whenever you are — day or night. Cost, waiting lists and office hours aren't barriers.

Getting more people into good, secure work is a national priority — and it shouldn't be held back by who can afford support. Whether you're a school leaver, a graduate, or returning to work after redundancy, caring, illness or a break, your career team can help you build a CV, make sense of job adverts and apply, prepare for interviews with realistic practice, and stay steady through the ups and downs.

Career Agenting System offers practical help and encouragement, but it isn't a substitute for regulated careers, legal, financial or benefits advice, and it isn't a therapy or crisis service. If you're struggling to cope, please talk to your GP, or contact Samaritans free on 116 123 (UK) any time.
