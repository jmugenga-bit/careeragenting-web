Returning to work

Getting back to work

Coming back to work after time away is a big step — and a normal one. Whatever the reason for your break, you can rebuild your confidence and your CV, and take it one step at a time. Here's how, for free.

A break doesn't define you

Time out of work — for redundancy, caring, health, raising a family, or anything else — is far more common than job adverts make it feel. Employers see it all the time. What matters is how you present where you are now and what you bring, not that there's a gap.

Explaining a gap on your CV

Be honest and brief. You don't owe anyone a detailed explanation. A short, factual line is usually enough, and you can frame any skills you kept up or gained during the time — organising, budgeting, caring, volunteering, learning. Focus the rest of the CV on what you can do for the employer now.

Rebuilding confidence step by step

Confidence comes back with small wins, not all at once. Start by getting your CV into shape, then apply for one or two roles that genuinely fit rather than dozens at random. Preparing properly for an interview — practising your answers out loud — does more for nerves than anything else.

Frequently asked questions

How do I explain a gap in my CV?

Be honest and brief — a short factual line is enough. Note any skills you kept up or gained (organising, caring, volunteering, learning), and focus the rest of your CV on what you can offer now.

Is Career Agenting System really free?

Yes — it's free to start every day with no sign-up. There's an optional Week Pass if you want more, but you can get real help without paying anything.

Will using this affect my benefits?

No — using free career help doesn't affect benefits. How taking up work affects a benefits claim is a question for your Jobcentre Plus work coach, who can advise on your specific situation.

I've been out of work a long time — is it too late?

No. Employers hire people returning to work all the time. Start by getting your CV into shape and applying for one or two roles that fit, and build from there.
