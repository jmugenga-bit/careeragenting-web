A free alternative to Zety

Zety is a well-known, polished résumé builder — but many people look for an alternative once they reach the download step. Here's an honest comparison.

WWren · CV SScout · applications AAce · interviews JJuno · support CCedar · growing in the job

Zety is an online résumé and cover-letter builder with a large library of templates and content prompts. It's genuinely good at quick, guided formatting and a professional-looking result.

What Zety does well

  • A big library of polished, professional templates
  • Guided, quick formatting with content prompts
  • A well-established, widely-used product

Why people look for a Zety alternative

  • It's free to build but paid to download — you generally need a paid plan to export as PDF or Word (plain text is free), which surprises many people at the final step.
  • It's a résumé builder — no interview practice or support through the search.
  • It's a global product rather than UK-focused.

Frequently asked questions

Is Zety free?

Zety is free to build a CV, but downloading it as a PDF or Word file generally needs a paid plan (plain-text download is free). Check Zety's site for current pricing.

What is a good free alternative to Zety?

Career Agenting System is free to start with no sign-up and no download paywall. It builds and tailors your CV and adds application, interview and support help — though it doesn't offer Zety's designed templates.

Why do people look for a Zety alternative?

Most commonly because they reach the download step and find they need to pay. Some also want interview help or UK-specific guidance, which a résumé builder doesn't provide.
