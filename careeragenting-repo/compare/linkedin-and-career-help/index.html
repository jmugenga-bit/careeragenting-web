LinkedIn and Career Agenting System — networking meets getting hired

LinkedIn is where professional networking, visibility and a lot of hiring happen — you should absolutely be on it. But a LinkedIn profile isn't a tailored UK CV, and LinkedIn won't run you through a mock interview. A free career team does that.

LinkedIn is the world's largest professional network, with job listings, easy-apply, recruiter visibility and a paid Premium tier that adds insights and some interview-prep tools. For networking, being found, and staying visible to recruiters, it's essential.

What LinkedIn does well

  • Professional networking and visibility to recruiters
  • Job listings and easy apply
  • A profile that helps people find you (plus Premium insights)

What LinkedIn doesn't do

  • A profile isn't a tailored, role-specific UK CV — you often still need one.
  • It doesn't decode a specific advert and tailor your application to it.
  • Its interview help is limited compared with structured mock practice, and it's not there for the emotional side.

Frequently asked questions

Is a LinkedIn profile the same as a CV?

No. A LinkedIn profile helps recruiters find you, but most UK applications still need a tailored CV for the specific role. Wren builds and tailors that — free.

Do I need LinkedIn Premium to get a job?

No. LinkedIn's free tier covers networking and applying; Premium adds insights and some interview tools. Career Agenting System adds tailored CV help and full mock interviews for free.

How do I use LinkedIn and Career Agenting System together?

Use LinkedIn to network, be visible and find roles; use your career team to tailor your CV to each one, prepare your interview answers, and stay supported.
