Frequently asked questions

Is this an alternative to Totaljobs?

No — it complements it. Totaljobs is where you find and apply to jobs; Career Agenting System helps you win them with CV, application and interview help. Both free.

How do I improve my Totaljobs applications?

Tailor each one to the advert rather than sending a generic CV. Scout decodes the advert so you can match what the employer wants — free.

Can it prepare me for the interview?

Yes — Ace predicts likely questions for the role and runs mock interviews so you can practise your answers.
