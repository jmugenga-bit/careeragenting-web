Reed and Career Agenting System — find the job, then win it

Reed.co.uk is one of the UK's best-known places to find work, with a huge range of roles. It's great for finding and applying — but not for writing a standout CV or preparing for the interview. That's where a free career team helps.

Reed is one of the largest UK job boards, listing roles across every sector, with easy apply and job alerts, plus a big library of courses. For finding UK vacancies, it's excellent and free.

What Reed does well

  • A large range of live UK vacancies across sectors
  • Easy applications, job alerts and saved searches
  • A well-known, trusted UK brand, plus courses

What a job board doesn't do

  • It lists jobs, but doesn't write or tailor your CV to a specific role.
  • It doesn't run mock interviews or predict likely questions.
  • It doesn't support you through the knock-backs and the waiting.

Is this an alternative to Reed?

No — it works alongside Reed. Reed is where you find and apply to UK jobs; Career Agenting System helps you win them with a tailored CV, application help and interview practice. Both are free.

Tailor your CV and application to each advert. Scout decodes what the employer is really asking for so you can match it — free, no sign-up.
