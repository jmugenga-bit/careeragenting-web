The Workington job market

Workington's economy grew from heavy industry, and manufacturing and engineering remain central today. The town and wider west Cumbria also sit within a significant energy and nuclear supply chain — the Sellafield site and its network of contractors are among the largest sources of skilled work in the region — alongside the port, construction, retail and public services.

For jobseekers, that means real demand for practical, technical and trade skills, plus the administrative and support roles that keep large employers running.
