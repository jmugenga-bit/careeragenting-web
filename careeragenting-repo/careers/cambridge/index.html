Career help in Cambridge

Cambridge is a world-leading science and technology cluster — strong in biotech, software and deep tech, where demonstrable skill wins.

WWren · CV SScout · applications AAce · interviews JJuno · support CCedar · growing in the job

Cambridge is one of the UK's most research-intensive economies, anchored by a world-class university and the 'Silicon Fen' cluster of technology, life-sciences and deep-tech employers. Employers value intellectual rigour, demonstrable skill and genuine curiosity, and the market rewards candidates who can show real, substantive work.

The Cambridge job market

Technology & softwareA dense cluster of software, AI, semiconductor and deep-tech firms.
Life sciences & biotechWorld-leading biotech, pharma and research employment.
Research & higher educationThe university and research institutes drive employment.
Professional & scientific servicesConsulting, IP and scientific-support firms.

Technology and life-sciences roles concentrate in the science and business parks around the city; research roles sit with the university and institutes. Lead with demonstrable projects, publications or technical depth — this is a market that rewards substance.

CV help in Cambridge

For technology and science roles, foreground projects, technical depth, tools and any publications or patents. For professional roles, lead with results and sector knowledge. Mirror the advert's exact terms so your CV passes screening in a specialist market.

Interview preparation in Cambridge

Expect rigorous technical or scientific interviews alongside competency questions — often a task, presentation or deep technical discussion. Prepare substantive examples and be ready to go into detail.

Job applications in Cambridge

Tailor each application to the role's real priorities. In a specialist market, precise, relevant depth beats breadth or volume.

Graduate and early-career jobs in Cambridge

Cambridge is a leading UK destination for science and technology graduates, with graduate and research routes across biotech, software and deep tech. Competition is high and roles are specialist, so demonstrable technical work matters.
