Career help in Reading

Reading is the heart of the Thames Valley technology corridor — one of the UK's strongest markets for IT, telecoms and professional services.

WWren · CV SScout · applications AAce · interviews JJuno · support CCedar · growing in the job

Reading anchors the Thames Valley, a leading UK cluster for technology, telecoms, IT services and professional and financial services, with many national and international headquarters nearby. Employers value technical capability, commercial awareness and clear results, and the market's competitiveness rewards candidates who tailor tightly to each role.

The Reading job market

Technology & telecomsA dense cluster of software, IT-services and telecoms employers.
Professional & financial servicesConsulting, insurance and professional firms across the corridor.
Business & shared servicesNational and international headquarters and operations centres.
Engineering & scienceTechnology-adjacent engineering and research employers.

Technology and corporate roles concentrate in the business parks and the town centre along the M4 corridor. Because many employers are well-known technology and professional-services names, commercial awareness and demonstrable skill matter as much as qualifications.

CV help in Reading

Interview preparation in Reading

Expect competency questions and, for technical roles, a task or technical discussion. Prepare specific, structured examples and be ready to explain why this employer and role.

Job applications in Reading

Tailor every application to the role's priorities — in a competitive corporate market, a focused, well-argued application beats volume.

Graduate and early-career jobs in Reading

Reading and the Thames Valley are strong for technology and business graduates, with graduate schemes at major tech and professional-services employers. The University of Reading and easy access to London widen early-career options.
