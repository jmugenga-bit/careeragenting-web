The Kendal job market

Kendal is the largest town in the old county of Westmorland and a hub for south Cumbria. Its economy mixes manufacturing — including food production and, historically, pharmaceuticals — with a distinctive outdoor and tourism sector (Kendal is closely tied to the outdoor industry and to Lake District visitors), plus retail, hospitality and professional services.

That blend means opportunities across very different fields, from production and logistics to customer-facing and office roles — and applications that clearly target the sector tend to win out.
