The Whitehaven job market

Whitehaven is a historic west-coast harbour town in Copeland, and the single biggest influence on its job market is the Sellafield nuclear site a few miles down the coast — one of the largest employers in the whole of Cumbria, supported by a deep network of contractors and supply-chain firms in engineering, construction, project management and specialist services.

Around that sit energy and engineering more broadly, the harbour, healthcare and public services, retail, and a tourism and heritage economy centred on the restored waterfront. The result is strong demand for skilled, technical and support roles.
