Greater Manchester

Career help in Manchester

Manchester has become one of the UK's fastest-growing city economies, with strong demand in media, digital, finance and life sciences — here's how to position yourself for it.

WWren · CV SScout · applications AAce · interviews JJuno · support CCedar · growing in the job

Manchester blends a large media and digital cluster with financial and professional services, a growing tech scene and a major university and health corridor. Employers here value people who can point to real projects and outcomes, and the city's collaborative, less-formal culture rewards candidates who come across as capable and easy to work with.

The Manchester job market

Media & digitalMediaCityUK in Salford anchors broadcasting, production and a wide digital cluster.
Finance & professional servicesSpinningfields and the city centre host banking, law and accountancy.
TechnologyA strong developer, data and e-commerce scene across the city.
Life sciences & healthThe Oxford Road corridor links universities, hospitals and research.

Creative and digital employers around MediaCityUK and the Northern Quarter respond to portfolios and demonstrable work; professional-services firms in Spinningfields expect polish and commercial awareness. Knowing which cluster your target role sits in helps you pitch the right way.

CV help in Manchester

For digital and media roles, put your best projects and measurable outcomes near the top and link to examples. For finance and professional roles, emphasise results, accuracy and progression. Use the advert's own keywords so your CV reads as a direct match.

Interview preparation in Manchester

Manchester interviews are often friendly but rigorous — expect competency questions and, in tech or creative roles, a practical task or portfolio review. Prepare a handful of strong, specific stories and show genuine interest in the team's work.

Job applications in Manchester

Tailoring matters more than volume. Decode each advert for the two or three priorities that will actually decide the shortlist, and make sure your application speaks to those directly.

Graduate and early-career jobs in Manchester

With several large universities, Manchester retains a lot of graduate talent, especially in digital, media, finance and life sciences. Graduate schemes at the bigger professional-services and tech employers are competitive, but the city's startup and agency scene is a strong route into early-career roles too.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I get free CV help in Manchester?

Career Agenting System is free to start and builds a tailored CV in minutes, and the National Careers Service offers free advice to UK adults. Manchester libraries and Jobcentre Plus also run free support sessions.

What industries is Manchester known for?

Media and digital (around MediaCityUK), finance and professional services, technology, and life sciences and healthcare along the Oxford Road corridor.

How do I prepare for interviews in Manchester?

Prepare specific examples using a situation-action-result structure, research the employer, and for tech or creative roles be ready for a practical task or portfolio review.

Is Manchester good for graduate jobs?

Yes — it retains strong graduate talent in digital, media, finance and life sciences, with both structured graduate schemes and a lively startup and agency scene for early-career roles.

Which areas of Manchester have the most jobs?

Media and digital roles cluster around MediaCityUK in Salford and the Northern Quarter; finance and professional services concentrate in Spinningfields and the city centre.
