The Penrith job market

Penrith sits just off the M6 at the gateway to the northern Lake District and Ullswater, and its economy reflects that. Agriculture and food production run deep across the surrounding Eden countryside, tourism and hospitality draw on the town's position as a Lakes gateway, and retail, distribution and public services provide steady local employment. The M6 junction makes it a natural spot for logistics too.

Because Penrith is a smaller market than Carlisle, roles turn over less often — which means when the right vacancy appears, a precise, well-tailored application matters even more.
