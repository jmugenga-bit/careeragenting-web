The Barrow-in-Furness job market

Barrow-in-Furness is dominated by BAE Systems Submarines: the Dreadnought and Astute programmes make the shipyard one of the largest single-site engineering employers in the UK, recruiting and training thousands of people — including major apprenticeship and graduate intakes — across engineering, manufacturing, project management and support functions.

Around it sit advanced manufacturing and defence engineering in the supply chain, the port, and a growing offshore-wind operations and maintenance sector serving the Irish Sea wind farms, plus healthcare, retail and public services. Demand for skilled trades, engineers and support staff is consistently strong.
