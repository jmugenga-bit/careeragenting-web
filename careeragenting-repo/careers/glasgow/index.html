Career help in Glasgow

Glasgow pairs a strong engineering and public-sector base with a fast-growing financial services and technology scene — here's how to position yourself locally.

WWren · CV SScout · applications AAce · interviews JJuno · support CCedar · growing in the job

Glasgow is Scotland's largest city economy, with deep roots in engineering and manufacturing and a modern core of financial services, technology and life sciences. Employers value practical capability and a straightforward, get-things-done attitude, and the city's collaborative culture rewards candidates who communicate clearly.

The Glasgow job market

Financial & business servicesThe International Financial Services District concentrates banking and operations.
Engineering & manufacturingA long heritage in engineering, now spanning energy and advanced manufacturing.
Public sector & healthLarge NHS, government and higher-education employers.
Technology & life sciencesA growing software, data and biosciences cluster.

Financial and business-services roles sit in the IFSD and city centre; engineering and energy employers spread across the wider region. Let the role's sector guide whether you emphasise commercial results or technical delivery.

CV help in Glasgow

For financial and business roles, lead with results and progression. For engineering, foreground technical standards, safety and delivered improvements. Match the advert's keywords closely so your CV passes both human and automated screening.

Interview preparation in Glasgow

Expect competency-based questions and, for technical roles, a scenario or task. Prepare specific examples and show genuine knowledge of the employer and the local sector.

Job applications in Glasgow

Tailor each application to the role's real priorities. A clear, focused cover note beats a generic one every time.

Graduate and early-career jobs in Glasgow

Glasgow's universities feed a steady graduate pipeline into financial services, engineering, technology and the public sector. Scotland's public careers service supports graduates too, and the city's growing tech and life-sciences clusters offer early-career routes alongside traditional graduate schemes.

Recent graduate? University of Glasgow runs a careers and employability service for its students and alumni — University of Glasgow. Our free graduate & student guides cover CVs with no experience, graduate schemes, assessment centres and interviews.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I get free CV help in Glasgow?

Career Agenting System is free to start and tailors your CV, and Scotland's public careers service and the National Careers Service offer free advice. Glasgow libraries and Jobcentre Plus also help.

What industries is Glasgow strong in?

Financial and business services, engineering and manufacturing, the public sector and health, and a growing technology and life-sciences scene.

How do I prepare for a job interview in Glasgow?

Prepare specific situation-action-result examples, research the employer and local sector, and for technical roles expect a task or scenario.

Is Glasgow good for graduates?

Yes — its universities feed graduates into financial services, engineering, technology and the public sector, with graduate schemes and a growing tech and life-sciences scene.

Where are most jobs in Glasgow?

Financial and business-services roles concentrate in the International Financial Services District and city centre; engineering and energy roles spread across the wider region.
