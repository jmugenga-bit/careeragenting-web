Greater London

Career help in London

London's job market is the largest and most competitive in the UK — which means a sharp, well-targeted CV and confident interview answers matter more here than almost anywhere.

WWren · CV SScout · applications AAce · interviews JJuno · support CCedar · growing in the job

London spans nearly every industry at scale, from global finance and law to technology, media, government and the creative arts. Roles cluster by district, and employers see a high volume of applicants — so the candidates who get shortlisted are usually the ones who tailor tightly to the specific role rather than sending a generic CV across the city.

The London job market

Finance & professional servicesThe City and Canary Wharf host banking, insurance, accountancy and law.
TechnologyA dense startup and scale-up scene, with big-tech hubs around King's Cross and Shoreditch.
Media & creativeAdvertising, publishing, film and design concentrate in the West End and Soho.
Government & public sectorCentral government, healthcare and large charities employ across the capital.

Where you apply often shapes how you should present yourself: the Square Mile and Canary Wharf reward precision and commercial awareness; the tech and creative clusters around Old Street, King's Cross and Soho value portfolios and demonstrable projects over formality.

CV help in London

For finance and professional roles, lead with measurable results and commercial impact. For tech and creative roles, foreground the projects, tools and outcomes you're proud of, and link to a portfolio where relevant. Because London employers screen high volumes, mirror the exact language of the advert so applicant-tracking systems and busy recruiters both spot the match.

Interview preparation in London

London interviews often include competency questions, a commercial or technical task, and several rounds. Prepare structured examples using a clear situation-action-result shape, and be ready to explain why this employer and this team — generic enthusiasm rarely survives a competitive process.

Job applications in London

Expect to tailor every application. A strong cover note that names the role's real priorities — decoded from the advert — will do more than volume. Quality over quantity is the winning strategy in a market this crowded.

Graduate and early-career jobs in London

London draws graduates from across the country into finance, law, tech, media and government graduate schemes, which are competitive and often close early — so prepare applications well ahead of deadlines. Many entry-level roles never appear on the big boards, so networking and speculative applications matter here.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I get free CV help in London?

You can use Career Agenting System free to start to build and tailor your CV, and the National Careers Service offers free careers advice and CV guidance to UK adults. Many London boroughs and libraries also run free job-search sessions.

How competitive is the London job market?

Very — employers typically receive large numbers of applications, so tailoring your CV and cover note to each specific role is the single biggest thing you can do to stand out.

Which industries are hiring in London?

London recruits across finance, technology, media, law, government and healthcare year-round. Focus on the sector that fits your experience and tailor tightly to each advert.

What are the main industries in London?

Finance and professional services, technology, media and creative, law, government and the public sector, and healthcare — spread across distinct districts from the City to King's Cross.

Can I apply for London jobs if I don't live there?

Yes — many London employers now offer hybrid or remote arrangements, and plenty of candidates commute. Be clear about your availability and, if relevant, your plans to relocate.
