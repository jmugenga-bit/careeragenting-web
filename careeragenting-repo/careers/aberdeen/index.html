Career help in Aberdeen

Aberdeen is the UK's energy capital, moving from oil and gas into renewables and the energy transition — here's how to position yourself for it.

WWren · CV SScout · applications AAce · interviews JJuno · support CCedar · growing in the job

Aberdeen's economy is built on energy — oil, gas and increasingly offshore wind, hydrogen and the wider energy transition — supported by strong engineering, subsea and professional services. Employers value technical capability, safety awareness and adaptability, and the market rewards candidates who can show real, delivered results.

The Aberdeen job market

EnergyOil and gas plus a growing offshore-wind, hydrogen and energy-transition sector.
Engineering & subseaWorld-class subsea, offshore and precision engineering.
Professional & business servicesLegal, financial and operational support for the energy sector.
Higher education & researchTwo universities and energy-research institutes.

Energy and engineering roles cluster around the harbour, the energy parks and the wider region; professional-services firms support them from the city centre. Emphasise technical rigour, safety and delivery, and show awareness of the energy transition.

CV help in Aberdeen

For energy and engineering, foreground technical standards, safety, certifications and delivered results. For professional roles, lead with results and sector knowledge. Match the advert's terms so your CV passes screening, and reflect transferable skills if you're moving between energy sub-sectors.

Interview preparation in Aberdeen

Expect competency questions and, for technical roles, a scenario or task. Prepare specific examples, show safety awareness, and demonstrate genuine understanding of where the energy sector is heading.

Job applications in Aberdeen

Tailor each application to the role's real priorities. As the sector shifts toward renewables, framing transferable skills clearly can open new routes.

Graduate and early-career jobs in Aberdeen

Aberdeen's two universities and its energy-research base make it strong for engineering, geoscience and energy graduates, with graduate routes across both traditional energy and the growing renewables and transition sectors.
