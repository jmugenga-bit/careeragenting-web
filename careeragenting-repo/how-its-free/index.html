How it's free — and how we make money

We think you should know exactly how a free service pays for itself. Here's the honest, simple version.

Free to start

You can use Career Agenting System free to start, with no sign-up and no card. For most people, the free help is genuinely enough to build a CV, tailor an application and prepare for an interview.

The optional Week Pass

If you're in an intensive stretch of job hunting and want more, there's an optional Week Pass for £9.99. It's a choice, never a wall in front of the basics — the core help stays free. There's nothing to cancel, because it isn't a rolling subscription.

What we don't do

We don't show ads, and we don't sell your data. Our aim is to keep the essential help free for everyone and let the optional pass support the running costs. That's the whole model — no hidden catch, no download paywall, no surprise charges.

Why free matters to us

The people who most need career help are often the least able to pay for it. Keeping the core free is the point of the whole thing — see our free career help page for more.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a catch to it being free?

No — the core help is free to start with no sign-up, no ads and no selling of your data. An optional £9.99 Week Pass is available if you want more, but the basics stay free.

Will I be charged automatically?

No — the Week Pass is a one-off optional choice, not a rolling subscription, so there's nothing to cancel and no surprise renewals.

Do you sell my data or show ads?

No to both. We don't sell your data and we don't run ads.
